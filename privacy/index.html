WeatherTop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

WeatherTop does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Nothing leaves your Mac.

What WeatherTop never does

  • No network access. WeatherTop never connects to the internet.
  • No screen reading. It does not capture, record or inspect what's on your screen, and it never reads window titles or contents.
  • No keystroke or input monitoring. It never sees what you type or click.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.

What it uses on your Mac, and why

To make the weather land on your windows and respond to you, WeatherTop reads a few things from macOS while it runs. They are used on the spot, never saved and never sent anywhere:

  • Window positions and sizes, so rain and snow can land on the top edges of your windows. macOS also reports which process owns each window; WeatherTop uses that only to ignore its own windows.
  • Your pointer's position, so moving it stirs the weather.
  • How long since you last used the mouse, keyboard or trackpad (a single number, not what you did), only if you turn on Start When Idle.
  • Whether your displays are asleep, your screen is locked, or another app is keeping the display awake (for example while a video plays), so the weather pauses when it shouldn't be showing.
  • The keyboard shortcut ⌃⌥⌘W, only if you turn it on. macOS tells WeatherTop only when that one shortcut is pressed.

Sounds are generated on your Mac as the weather happens; there are no recordings and no microphone access.

What it saves

Your preferences (weather type, intensity, wind, sound and volume, idle settings, which displays show weather, and similar options) are saved in WeatherTop's sandboxed preferences on your Mac. To identify your displays it saves their system identifiers. WeatherTop also keeps a simple count of how many days and how long you've used it, so it asks for an App Store rating at most once per version. None of this leaves your Mac, and deleting the app removes it.

Ratings

If WeatherTop asks you to rate it, the prompt is shown by macOS and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. WeatherTop doesn't learn whether or how you rated it.
